LOW-CARB MAIN DISHES

Low-Carb Chicken & Vegetable Stir-Fry

A four-serving skillet meal built around one full 20-ounce bag of Great Value Deluxe Stir-Fry Vegetables, one pound of protein, and a homemade savory garlic-ginger-sesame sauce. No regular rice.

Prep: About 15 minutesCook: About 15–20 minutesYield: 4 servingsBase: No rice

Ingredients

  • 1 (20-ounce) bag Great Value Deluxe Stir-Fry Vegetables
  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breast, thinly sliced (beef, shrimp, or a combination may be substituted)
  • 1 tablespoon neutral cooking oil
  • ⅓ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • ¼ cup chicken broth
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1–2 teaspoons zero-calorie sweetener, optional
  • Crushed red pepper flakes, optional

Instructions

  1. Mix the sauce. Whisk together the soy sauce, chicken broth, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and optional sweetener and red pepper flakes. Set aside.
  2. Cook the protein. Heat the neutral cooking oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the thinly sliced chicken in batches and cook until fully cooked through. Transfer to a plate. If substituting beef or shrimp, cook that protein until done and transfer it to a plate.
  3. Cook the vegetables. Add the entire 20-ounce bag of Great Value Deluxe Stir-Fry Vegetables to the skillet. Stir-fry until heated through and crisp-tender, following package directions if the vegetables require a different cooking method.
  4. Combine. Return the cooked protein to the skillet. Pour in the sauce and toss to coat.
  5. Finish. Simmer for 2–3 minutes, stirring, until everything is hot and the sauce lightly coats the protein and vegetables.
  6. Serve. Divide into four servings. Serve as-is, or with cauliflower rice or shredded cabbage. Skip regular rice to keep the meal low-carb.

Protein choices

The one-pound protein portion can be chicken, beef, shrimp, or a combination. The nutrition estimate below uses thin-sliced boneless, skinless chicken breast as the reference protein.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving · ¼ of recipe, chicken version; no cauliflower rice or cabbage included

265 calories  |  35 g protein  |  11 g fat  |  8 g carbohydrates

Nutrition is an estimate based on typical ingredient labels and the chicken version. Beef, shrimp, protein combinations, and optional ingredients will change the values. The recipe is a kitchen-test draft; confirm vegetable texture, sauce balance, and final nutrition after cooking.
