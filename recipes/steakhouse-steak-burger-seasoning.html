Steakhouse Steak & Burger Seasoning

A savory homemade blend for adding steakhouse-style flavor to steaks and burgers.

Prep: A few minutesCook: NoneYield: To be confirmed

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons smoked paprika
  • 1½ tablespoons ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground mustard
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder

Instructions

  1. Measure all ingredients into a small bowl.
  2. Stir thoroughly until the spices are evenly combined.
  3. Transfer to a clean, dry, airtight seasoning container.

Nutrition
