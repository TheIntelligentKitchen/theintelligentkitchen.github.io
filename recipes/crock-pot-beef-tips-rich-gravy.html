Crock Pot Beef Tips with Rich Gravy

Tender slow-cooked beef in savory brown gravy with onion and optional mushrooms. Best served over mashed potatoes or egg noodles.

Prep: Not specifiedSlow cook: LOW · 6–8 hoursGravy finish: HIGH · 20–30 minutesYield: 4–6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 lb beef stew meat or beef tips, cut into roughly 1-inch pieces
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced (optional)
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 packet brown gravy mix
  • 1 packet onion soup mix
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced, or 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp butter or cooking oil, for browning
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 3 tbsp cold water

Instructions

  1. Brown the beef. Heat the butter or oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season the beef with black pepper and brown it in batches, getting a good crust on the outside. It does not need to cook through.
  2. Add everything to the slow cooker. Add the browned beef, onion, mushrooms if using, beef broth, brown gravy mix, onion soup mix, Worcestershire sauce, and garlic. Stir together.
  3. Cook until tender. Cover and cook on LOW for 6–8 hours, until the beef is fork-tender.
  4. Thicken the gravy. About 20–30 minutes before serving, mix the cornstarch and cold water until smooth. Stir the slurry gently into the slow cooker. Turn the cooker to HIGH, cover, and cook 20–30 minutes, until the gravy thickens.

Serving suggestion

Best served over mashed potatoes or egg noodles.

Nutrition
