Savory Cottage Cheese & Cheddar Egg Bites

A savory 12-cup batch with the same cottage-cheese-and-cheddar egg mixture in every bite: six spinach-cheddar and six plain-cheddar. No mushrooms.

Prep: About 10 minutesBake: 22–27 minutesOven: 325°FYield: 12 egg bites

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 1 cup 4% cottage cheese
  • 1 cup triple-cheddar shredded cheese
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • ¼ teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 (13.5-ounce) can Popeye Leaf Spinach, drained very well, for the six spinach-cheddar bites

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Grease all 12 cups of a standard muffin pan well.
  2. Blend or whisk the eggs and cottage cheese until evenly combined.
  3. Stir in the triple-cheddar cheese, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika.
  4. Divide the egg mixture evenly among the 12 muffin cups.
  5. For six spinach-cheddar bites, distribute the very well-drained spinach among six cups and gently stir it into the mixture in those cups. Leave the other six cups plain-cheddar.
  6. Bake for 22–27 minutes, until the centers are set. Let rest briefly, then loosen the edges and remove from the pan.

Two varieties in one pan

Make six spinach-cheddar bites and six plain-cheddar bites from the same egg mixture and seasoning blend. The spinach is added only to its six cups. Mushrooms are not included.
