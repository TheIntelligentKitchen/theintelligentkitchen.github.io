Old-Fashioned Bacon Gravy

Creamy, old-fashioned country gravy made with bacon grease, a simple flour roux, milk, and plenty of black pepper. Serve over warm biscuits with crispy bacon.

Prep: Not specifiedCook: 5–8 minutes after rouxYield: To be confirmed

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons bacon grease
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper, or to taste
  • Salt, only if needed

Instructions

  1. Cook bacon and leave the browned bits in the pan. Remove excess grease, leaving 3 tablespoons.
  2. Heat the bacon grease over medium heat.
  3. Add the flour and stir for 1–2 minutes to make a roux.
  4. Gradually whisk in the milk until smooth.
  5. Cook for 3–5 minutes, stirring, until the gravy thickens.
  6. Stir in black pepper. Taste before adding salt; add salt only if needed.

Kitchen notes

  • Fresh-ground black pepper works especially well.
  • If the gravy gets too thick, thin it with a little milk.
  • For thicker gravy, let it simmer a little longer.
  • The goal is creamy gravy that clings to a biscuit, not a pasty texture.

Serving suggestion: spoon over warm biscuits and serve with crispy bacon.
